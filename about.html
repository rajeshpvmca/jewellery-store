Genesis of an Heirloom

Where British Architectural Precision Meets Timeless Brilliance

Founded in 1888 on London’s illustrious New Bond Street, Maison Stackly began as an atelier dedicated solely to bespoke commissions for European aristocrats and connoisseurs of rare natural gemstones.

Through four generations of master jewellers, our core conviction has remained unwavering: every diamond possesses an innate soul, requiring patient lapidary mastery and micro-pave craftsmanship to reveal its eternal fire.

138 Years of Distinction

Milestones in High Jewellery

A chronological odyssey through iconic commissions and international acclaim.

1888

The Mayfair Doors Open

Charles Stackly opens the first private appointment salon on New Bond Street, specializing in bespoke emerald and diamond chokers.

1925

The Art Deco Crown Commission

Commissioned to create a platinum and Colombian emerald bandeau for an English ducal wedding, heralding our geometric deco era.

1974

GIA Fellowship & Diamond Vault Expansion

Stackly establishes London’s premier private subterranean diamond vault with state-of-the-art optical spectroscopy grading.

2026

The Sovereign High Jewellery Collection

Unveiling our most ambitious collection to date: 24 one-of-a-kind masterworks combining 3D CAD modeling with hand-carved heritage techniques.

Responsible Luxury

Pure Provenance, Uncompromised Conscience

At Maison Stackly, luxury is defined not merely by scintillation, but by the integrity with which a gemstone journeys from the earth to our Mayfair workbench.

We rigorously enforce the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPCS) and work exclusively with compliant artisanal mines in Botswana, Canada, and Australia that uphold fair living wages and environmental reclamation.

  • 100% Conflict-free certified natural diamonds
  • Recycled 950 platinum and Fairmined certified gold
  • Zero chemical synthetic color enhancements
