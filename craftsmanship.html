Stone Setting Mastery

The Five Disciplines of the Gem-Setter

Master setters use stereomicroscopes at 40x magnification to raise metal beads with surgical precision.

Micro-Pavé Setting

Minute gemstones placed within microscopically carved honeycomb seats, held by rounded micro-grains to create a carpet of seamless fire.

Royal Claw & Prong

Hand-filed talon and eagle-claw prongs engineered to grip the diamond's girdle securely while exposing maximum pavilion facets to ambient light.

Bezel & Collet Setting

A continuous ribbon of 950 platinum or 18K gold hand-rubbed over the gemstone rim, providing unmatched everyday protection and sleek modern elegance.

Channel Setting

Precision-cut baguette and princess diamonds suspended edge-to-edge between parallel metal walls, creating sleek, flush architectural lines.

Invisible Setting

Our pinnacle technique where grooves are cut into the pavilion of calibrated stones and slid onto an internal gold rail with zero visible surface metal.

Gypsy & Flush Setting

Stones embedded directly into solid metal bands with a polished burnished rim, celebrating tactile minimalism for everyday luxury.

Digital Precision meets Ancient Metallurgy

From Aerospace 3D CAD to Centrifugal Lost-Wax Pouring

We unite centuries-old foundry traditions with cutting-edge aerospace 3D modeling. Every high jewellery mounting is first drafted to micron tolerances in CAD, calculating the exact stone girdle depth to avoid stress fractures.

The model is 3D printed in combustible casting wax, encased in refractory ceramic plaster, and vaporized in kilns at 750°C. Molten platinum or gold is then centrifugally forced into the resulting void, forging an uninterrupted monolithic chassis.

Microscopic Scrutiny

Multi-Tier Mirror Polishing & Quality Audits

Before any creation leaves our Mayfair atelier, it undergoes three separate polishing phases using dialux rouge compounds, walnut shell tumblers, and hand-held cotton mops running at 3,000 RPM.

Each prong is individually inspected under 100x stereomicroscopes to ensure zero tool marks, flawless curvature, and rock-solid stone immobilization.
