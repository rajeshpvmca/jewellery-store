The Creation Process

The Bespoke 5-Step Atelier Journey

From initial gouache sketches to the London Assay Office hallmark, follow your heirloom's genesis.

01
Gouache Sketch

Hand-painted watercolor sketches exploring stone geometry and ergonomic finger contours.

02
Gem Sourcing

Ethical procurement of certified rough and polished stones from Botswana, Colombia, and Ceylon.

03
3D Wax Cast

Micro-precision CAD engineering translated into tactile wax prototypes for finger fitting.

04
Goldsmith Forging

Hand-setting stones under 40x stereomicroscopes into forged 950 platinum or 18K gold.

05
Assay Hallmark

Official British assay testing, London Leopard hallmark stamp, and white-glove delivery.
