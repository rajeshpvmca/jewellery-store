Inquiries to the Atelier

Ask the Master Gemologist

Answers to our discerning clientele's most intricate gemstone queries.

Q: Does diamond fluorescence affect brilliance in sunlight?

A: Medium to strong blue fluorescence can make faint yellow diamonds (I-J color) appear noticeably whiter in daylight. However, in D-F colorless diamonds, excessive fluorescence may cause a milky haze, which is why Maison Stackly strictly filters for 'None' to 'Faint' fluorescence.

Q: What is the difference between Fairmined and Recycled Gold?

A: Recycled gold re-purifies existing jewellery scrap to reduce virgin mining, whereas Fairmined gold directly supports certified artisanal mining cooperatives that adhere to strict ecological standards and social equity wages in South America.
